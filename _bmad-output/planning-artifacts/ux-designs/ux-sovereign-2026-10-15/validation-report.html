Executive Summary

The Sovereign UX spine pair (DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md) and companion interactive mockups were evaluated across three specialized lenses: Rubric Walker, Accessibility & Usability Lens, and Responsive & Viewport Lens.

Zero critical architectural flaws were found. All 8 findings across traceability, contrast, focus states, and responsive scaling were resolved directly into the final spines and companion HTML mockups.

1. Flow Coverage & Traceability Strong

Ensures 100% parity with PRD user journeys with named protagonists, numbered steps, climaxes, and failure paths.

Resolved Traceability Harmonization: Updated all 5 journey titles in EXPERIENCE.md to mirror prd.md verbatim (UJ-1 through UJ-5).
2. Color Contrast & Tokens Strong

WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance across text, backgrounds, and interactive controls.

Resolved Faint Text Contrast: Darkened text-faint token from #94A3B8 to #475569, lifting contrast ratio to 7.0:1 (exceeding WCAG AAA).
3. Color Independence & Status Micro-Shapes Strong

Status indicators must not rely on color alone (WCAG 1.4.1).

Resolved Geometric Micro-Shapes: Paired semantic colors with distinct shapes: Green circle (●), Amber diamond (◆), Red triangle (▲).
4. Keyboard Focus & Screen Reader Semantics Strong

WCAG 2.1.1, 2.4.7 focus visibility and ARIA attributes.

Resolved Focus Rings & ARIA: Added explicit :focus-visible styles (2px solid #2563EB), labeled toggle switches via aria-labelledby, and added aria-live="polite" for telemetry.
5. Responsive Viewports & Extensibility Strong

Single-viewport physics (1080p desktop) and mobile touch targets (≥44px).

Resolved Extensible Grid Physics: Pinned header/telemetry bars while unlocking internal grid scroll for 5+ services; expanded mobile touch targets to ≥44px.
